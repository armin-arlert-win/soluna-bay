SOLUNA BAY is a 3D third-person open-world crime game built for [make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01): a sunny coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, carjacking, a 1–5 star wanted level with police chases, roadblocks and a helicopter, eight story missions with a crew, street races, combat, a garage, properties, collectibles, respect and rank, three radio stations, and saves. Vanilla JavaScript + raw WebGL, no build step, playable offline in desktop Chrome.

Run it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080 — or open `game/index.html` directly in Chrome.
